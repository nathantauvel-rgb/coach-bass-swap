What you buy

DJingood Pro unlocks all of DJingood: the seven chapters of exercises with your controller (beyond the first three levels, which are free), Expert Mode on every level, practice (variations, by ear, mini-sets, quick quiz), "My music" (what goes with each track in your rekordbox collection, the recommended technique, the missions, the favorites), and all the tutorials in the "Tutorials" tab, plus the ones added later, at a pace of about one a month. Still free, with no subscription: the first three levels of chapter 1, the "Understand" lessons, the daily challenge, the "The fader fade" tutorial, and in "My music" importing your rekordbox collection and the card for one of your tracks. You need a DJingood account, an internet connection and a recent browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).

The free month for the first players

DJingood accounts created up to and including November 11, 2026 get their first month of Pro free, once per account. An account created after that date has access to the free exercises, without a free month. It starts without a credit card. During that month, you can add a card in "My account", then "Manage my subscription": the subscription then continues at the end of the month, at the price of the plan you chose. Without a card added, Pro stops on its own at the end of the month and nothing is charged.

The 7 free days

A DJingood account that has never had a subscription can try Pro for free for 7 days, once per account. Your credit card is requested at the start of the trial, but nothing is charged during those 7 days. Two days before the end, DJingood emails you to remind you of the date and the amount of the first payment. At the end of the 7 days, the plan you chose (€4.99 a month or €49.99 a year) starts on its own and the first payment is charged, unless you cancelled it before then (see "Cancelling"): Pro then closes at the end of the trial and nothing is charged.

Your first year for €34.99

A DJingood account that has never had a subscription and that opened the payment page without paying can, within the next 48 hours and only once, take the yearly plan at €34.99 for the first year instead of €49.99. The end date and time of the offer are shown on the site, and the offer can be combined with the 7 free days. At the end of that first year, the subscription renews at the regular price of the yearly plan, €49.99 a year, unless you cancel it before then.

Price and payment

Two plans: €4.99 a month or €49.99 a year, all taxes included. Payment is made in advance, at the start of each period, by credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through Stripe, which collects payments for DJingood: DJingood never sees your card. An invoice is emailed to you with each payment, and you can find them in "My account".

The subscription renews on its own at the end of each period, at the price of your plan (€49.99 a year after a first year at €34.99), until you cancel it. For the yearly plan, you get an email one to three months before the renewal date, reminding you that you can cancel. If the price ever changes, you'll be told at least 30 days in advance, and you can cancel before it applies.

Immediate access, and the right of withdrawal

Pro opens as soon as you pay (or as soon as the 7 free days, or the free month, begin). Since this is digital content that you start using right away, the law allows you to waive the 14-day right of withdrawal (article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code): when you pay, you expressly ask for it by ticking the box provided. Without that box, the subscription can't start.

Cancelling

Whenever you want, in two clicks: My account, then Manage my subscription, then "Cancel subscription". Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for: Pro stays open until then, and nothing more is charged afterwards. The period already started isn't refunded. During the 7 free days, it's the same: Pro stays open until the end of the trial, and nothing is charged.
